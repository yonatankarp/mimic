This update has 7 new features, 2 improvements and 5 bug fixes, plus more in Terminal.

### ✨ New features
- **Make a mini from several pictures.** Add the back and sides of your character, so the 3D model doesn't have to guess them (with TRELLIS.2).
- **Import a model you already have.** File → Import Model… makes a GLB or STL print-ready, with a base.
- **See a mini's real size.** The ruler in the 3D view puts a base, a person or a grid beside it.
- **Compare two versions side by side** in 3D, and keep the one you like.
- **Choose where your minis are kept** in Settings → General.
- **Report a Problem** in the Help menu gathers what a bug report needs, with your private details taken out.
- **More in the menu bar:** sort, filter and find minis, and pause the queue from the Dock.

### 🔧 Improvements
- Pictures are now made at a lower priority, so your Mac stays quick while a mini is made.
- Small touches: Duplicate can be undone, clearer settings, shorter tips.

### 🐞 Bug fixes
- ⌘N no longer replaces an open window and loses what you typed.
- Edit & Make Again starts at a mini's real size when its sizes weren't saved.
- Names keep their accents, including names taken from a description.
- The 3D view shows that it's loading when you switch minis.
- Queue times are more accurate for New 3D Shape and Try Again.

### ⌨️ In Terminal
- New commands: `open`, `info`, `rename`, `trash`, `keep`, `stop` and `project`.
- `--json` on `list`, `projects`, `queue`, `models` and `info`.
- Tab completion for zsh, bash and fish.
- `mimic resize` keeps the nozzle, and names show as you typed them.
